// about

        
A decade deep in offensive security.

        

          I have spent the better part of ten years finding the bugs that ship past code
          review and scanners. From WordPress plugins to enterprise GraphQL gateways to
          mobile apps with eight figure user counts, my work has been shipped, patched,
          and publicly disclosed across hundreds of programs.
        

        

          I work the way I would want a tester to work for my own product: manual first,
          source aware, with reports your engineers can act on without translation. No
          inflated severities, no vague paragraphs, no proprietary scanner outputs
          dressed up as findings. Reproducible PoCs, severity rationale you can defend in
          a triage meeting, and remediation guidance that ships.
        

        
    
          
  • Independent. No agency overhead, you talk to the operator who is testing your asset.
